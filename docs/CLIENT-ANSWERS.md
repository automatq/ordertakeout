# Your Online Ordering Website — Answers to Your Questions

Thanks for sending over your requirements. Below are direct answers to each of the nine questions you asked,
followed by a short list of details we need from you to get started.

**Short version:** yes to all nine. Because you already use Square, we can connect the website directly to your
Square account — so online orders show up on your Square POS the same way any other order does, already marked
as paid. On top of that we'll build you a simple order screen of your own, and send you an email and a text for
every order.

---

## 1. How will the store be notified when a customer places an order?

Four ways at the same time, so an order can't slip past you:

- **Your Square POS** — the order appears in Order Manager, just like a normal Square order
- **Email** — sent to whichever address you choose
- **Text message** — sent to the store phone
- **Your order screen** — a web page you can leave open on a tablet or computer in the kitchen

We can also post orders into a Discord or Slack channel, or create a card on a Trello board, if that's useful
for your team.

## 2. Will a new order automatically pop up or send a notification to the store?

Yes. The order screen refreshes on its own and plays a sound the moment a new order comes in, so nobody has to
keep checking it. Square POS also gives its own new-order alert and can automatically print a receipt if you
turn that on.

## 3. How can the staff see whether an order has already been paid online?

**Every order on the website is paid before it's confirmed** — there's no unpaid online order. On top of that,
Square is set up so an order only appears on your POS once the payment has actually cleared. So if you can see
it in Square, the money is already in.

Your order screen also shows a clear **PAID** badge, the amount, and how they paid (card, Apple Pay, etc.).

## 4. Will the system show separate statuses such as New Order, Paid, Preparing, Ready for Pickup, and Completed?

Yes — those exact statuses:

**New Order → Paid → Preparing → Ready for Pickup → Completed** (plus Canceled)

Staff move an order along with one tap. When you mark an order "Ready for Pickup," the customer automatically
gets a message telling them it's ready.

One small note: Square's own screen has one fewer step than your list — it doesn't have a separate "Preparing."
Your order screen tracks all five; Square will simply show those orders as active. Nothing is lost, the two
screens just label that middle step a little differently.

## 5. Where will all orders appear — a dashboard, email, mobile app, or POS system?

All of them:

- **Dashboard** — your own order screen, which works on a phone or tablet as well as a computer
- **Email** — full order details including pickup date and time
- **Phone** — a text message for each order
- **POS** — in Square, on the same device you already use

## 6. Can the store receive an email or phone notification every time a customer places and pays for an order?

Yes, both. They're sent the moment payment goes through — not when someone just adds something to their cart —
so you're only alerted about real, paid orders.

## 7. Can customers select their pickup date and time before checking out?

Yes. Picking a date and a pickup time is **required** — the pay button doesn't turn on until they've chosen. The
website only offers dates and times that are actually valid for what's in their cart.

## 8. Can the system prevent customers from ordering Ensaymada after the 6:00 PM cutoff for next-day pickup?

Yes. After 6:00 PM, tomorrow's pickup times simply disappear and the earliest date a customer can choose moves
to the day after. This is enforced on our server using your store's local time — so it works correctly no matter
what time zone the customer is in, and someone can't get around it by leaving the page open or changing the clock
on their phone.

## 9. Can the system limit the number of orders available for each pickup time to prevent overbooking?

Yes, in two ways, and you control both:

- **A limit per pickup time** — for example, no more than 5 orders at the 4:00 PM slot
- **A limit per day, per product** — for example, no more than 40 Ensaymada trays total on any one day

When a slot fills up it's greyed out and can't be chosen. You can change these limits yourself at any time, and
block off dates entirely for holidays or closures.

## What we need from you to get started

1. **Pickup rules for Hopia and Ube Bars.** Your notes give the exact rule for Ensaymada (order by 6:00 PM, pick
   up from 4:00 PM the next day). For Hopia and Ube Bars we just need the same three things: how far in advance
   people must order, the daily cutoff time, and which pickup times you want to offer.
2. **Your time zone**, so the 6:00 PM cutoff lands correctly.
3. **Are the prices you listed tax-included, or is tax added at checkout?**
4. **Your capacity numbers** — roughly how many trays you can make in a day, and how many orders you can
   comfortably hand out in one pickup hour. We can start with a safe number and adjust once you see real volume.
5. **Where notifications should go** — the email address and the mobile number for the store.
6. **Access to your Square account** so we can connect the website to it, and confirmation of whether these 8
   items already exist in your Square item library (if not, we'll add them).
7. **A domain name** for the website, plus your logo and any photos of the products you'd like us to use.
8. **Any order limits** — for example, whether there's a maximum a customer can order online before they need to
   call the store directly.

---

## What the first version will and won't include

**Included:** the 8 party tray items, pickup date and time selection, online payment (card, Apple Pay, Google
Pay, Cash App Pay), your order screen with alerts, email and text notifications, order confirmation emails for
customers, and a page where customers can check their order status.

**Not in the first version:** delivery, customer accounts/logins, discount codes, gift cards, and your regular
daily bakery menu for same-day walk-in pickup. We're building the system so the daily menu can be added later
without a rebuild — we just want to get party tray ordering working well first.
